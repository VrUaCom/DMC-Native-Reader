# Code of Conduct

DMC Native Reader welcomes technical discussion, bug reports, reverse-engineering evidence and contributions from people with different levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing code, evidence or mistakes.
- Critique claims and implementations, not people.
- Separate confirmed evidence from hypotheses.
- Give contributors room to correct errors without ridicule or harassment.
- Respect licensing, copyright and redistribution boundaries.
- Do not pressure others to share proprietary game files, leaked material, credentials or private data.
- Keep security-sensitive reports out of public threads until maintainers confirm disclosure is safe.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate doxxing, credential exposure, spam, malicious files, or attempts to use project infrastructure to distribute material without redistribution rights are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior materially harms contributors, users or project security.

For security-sensitive matters, follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). For ordinary project questions, use the issue templates and [`SUPPORT.md`](SUPPORT.md).
